# NEW SKILLS INTERNATIONAL ACADEMY CO., LTD.

**Trading Name:** New Skills International Academy
**Abbreviation:** NSIA
**Website:** nsi.ac
**Type of Activity:** Private online education and training academy
**Primary Business Activity:** Education and Training
**Mode of Delivery:** 100% Online
**Nature of Education:** Language, technical, information technology, vocational and professional skills courses

## 1. Nature of Activities

New Skills International Academy Co., Ltd. (“NSIA”) is a private education and training institution operating through an online learning model.

The purpose of the Academy is to provide accessible, modern and practical education to learners in Cambodia and internationally, regardless of their geographical location.

NSIA focuses primarily on the development of practical skills and competencies that learners can apply in further education, professional employment, entrepreneurship, business activities and personal development.

The Academy’s educational offering may include, in particular:

* foreign language education;
* information technology;
* computer programming;
* artificial intelligence;
* web technologies;
* cybersecurity;
* systems and network administration;
* graphic design and multimedia technologies;
* digital marketing;
* entrepreneurship and business skills;
* other modern digital, technical, vocational and professional skills.

NSIA does not operate as a higher education institution and does not award academic degrees such as Bachelor’s, Master’s or Doctoral degrees.

The Academy primarily provides short- and medium-term educational and training courses, upon completion of which learners may receive an internal certificate confirming successful completion of the relevant course.

---

# 2. Mission of the Academy

The mission of New Skills International Academy is to enable people to acquire new and practical skills regardless of their geographical location, age or previous educational pathway.

The fundamental idea of the Academy can be expressed as:

**Learn new skills. Create new opportunities.**

NSIA aims to combine language education, technology and professional skills within a single international learning environment.

The Academy places particular emphasis on practical knowledge and applicable skills.

The purpose of a course is not merely to provide information, but to enable the learner to reach a level at which the acquired knowledge and skills can be applied independently in practical situations.

---

# 3. International Character

New Skills International Academy has an international character.

The legal entity is established in Cambodia, while its educational activities are delivered through online technologies.

As a result:

# 5. Methods of Course Delivery

NSIA may provide courses through several online learning models.

## 5.1. Live Online Learning

Classes are conducted live by a teacher or instructor.

Learners participate at scheduled times and may communicate directly with the instructor, ask questions and complete exercises together with other participants.

This model may be particularly suitable for:

* language learning;
* conversation classes;
* programming;
* technical workshops;
* consultations;
* project-based classes.

## 5.2. Self-Paced Online Learning

Learners complete courses according to their own schedule and learning pace.

A course may include:

* video lessons;
* written materials;
* presentations;
* demonstrations and examples;
* exercises;
* virtual laboratories;
* quizzes;
* tests;
* practical projects.

The learning platform records and monitors the learner’s progress.

## 5.3. Instructor-Led Online Learning

Learners receive access to online learning materials while remaining under the supervision and guidance of an instructor.

The instructor may:

* answer questions;
* review assignments;
* assess projects;
* conduct consultations;
* monitor learner progress.

## 5.4. Mixed Online Learning

Courses may combine:

**self-study + live online classes + instructor consultations + practical projects + online assessments.**

This model may serve as the principal delivery method for more comprehensive NSIA programmes.

# 13. Certificates

Upon satisfying the completion requirements of a particular course, a learner may receive an:

**NSIA Certificate of Completion**

A certificate may contain:

* learner’s full name;
* course title;
* course level;
* total number of learning or instructional hours;
* date of completion;
* final result, where applicable;
* unique certificate number;
* QR code;
* electronic signature or signature of an authorised representative.

An example certificate number may be:

**NSIA-IT-PY101-2027-000001**

Each certificate may be independently verifiable through:

**nsi.ac/verify**

By entering the certificate number or scanning its QR code, a third party may verify the authenticity of the certificate.

An NSIA certificate confirms successful completion of a specific course and fulfilment of the requirements established by the Academy for that course.

It is not represented as an academic degree or government-issued diploma unless a specific programme subsequently receives appropriate external accreditation, recognition or authorisation.

# 24. Legal and Educational Nature of the Services

New Skills International Academy provides private online education and training services.

The Academy does not represent its courses as higher education degree programmes and does not award academic degrees.

Certificates issued by NSIA confirm successful completion of specific courses and fulfilment of the educational requirements established for those courses.

Where a particular programme may in the future obtain accreditation, recognition, certification or authorisation from an external institution or competent authority, such status will be communicated separately and only in relation to the specific programme to which it applies.

# 25. Summary of the NSIA Model

**New Skills International Academy Co., Ltd.**

**nsi.ac**

is an international online academy focused on the practical development of new skills.

The Academy’s educational model is based on five fundamental elements:

**Learn → Practice → Assess → Certify → Progress**

A learner selects a specific skill, completes a structured online course, undertakes practical exercises and assessments, demonstrates achievement of the required learning outcomes and receives verifiable confirmation of successful course completion.

The principal objective of NSIA is to create a modern educational environment accessible from anywhere in the world and combining:

**Languages + Technology + Artificial Intelligence + Digital Skills + Professional Skills.**
